# 4.1 Competitor research - WarrantyKhata
Researched 2026-09-20. India focus. Pages were fetched with the
fetch tool, which summarises the page; quotes below are what that tool returned, and "NOT ON PAGE" means
the page did not mention it - NOT proof the product lacks the feature (marketing pages do not list everything).

## Question
Who else solves "small mobile/electronics shops lose warranty, IMEI/serial and customer records after a sale"
in India, and is there a real gap (specifically: warranty-EXPIRY reminders for the shop)?

## Findings by group
**Direct - shop billing / mobile-shop software (already used at the counter)**
- Vyapar - stores IMEI per phone; "Link products with warranty periods to quickly verify coverage". Reminders on
  the page are payment reminders and "Service Reminders" (Gold plan); warranty-expiry reminder NOT ON PAGE.
  Mobile app free (Rs 0); paid Silver Rs 283/month billed annually + 18% GST.
- myBillBook - "link the unique IMEI number of every phone directly to its bill". No warranty-period feature and
  no expiry reminder on page. Plus plan Rs 291/month billed annually.
- KhataBuddy - IMEI/serial "traceable for returns, exchanges and warranty". No expiry reminder on page; no price on page.
- GoFinicky MobileShop POS - "Maintain device warranty details and complete customer service history"; IMEI tracking.
  No expiry reminder or WhatsApp/SMS on page. Dealer/distributor pricing, no figures.
- Zubizi - IMEI tracking; WhatsApp invoices and "service reminders". No warranty tracking or expiry reminder on page.

**Adjacent / indirect**
- Warranty Mitra - "warranty distribution platform for retailers & partners": retailers SELL extended warranty and
  protection plans and earn extra income. Different job (selling plans, not keeping the shop's records). No reminders on page.
- Traciqo - brand-side WhatsApp warranty registration by the customer; expiry reminders at 90, 30 and 7 days; US$59/month
  starter. Appears aimed at manufacturers/brands, priced in dollars.
- Warranty Book - buyer-side app: bills and warranty cards, expiry reminders for the buyer; one line about a "Business user" sharing a warranty card.
- Paper: carbon bill book, warranty register, bill photos in WhatsApp, memory of the customer's face (the founder's own
  12 years at a shop and the persona's "how he manages today" - lived observation, not a researched source).

## Gap (careful wording)
On the pages read, none of the five shop-software tools mention warranty-expiry reminders to the shop owner. Expiry
reminders DO exist at Traciqo (brand side, USD) and Warranty Book (buyer side). So the defensible claim is:
"we did not find a shop-side, low-typing, WhatsApp expiry reminder in rupee pricing on these companies' public pages" -
NOT "nobody does this". The earlier 3.3 slide line "nobody reminds the shop" is stronger than this evidence.
**Biggest real risk:** Rajesh already uses GST billing software; Vyapar or myBillBook could add expiry reminders as a
feature. Price anchor: their paid plans start near Rs 283-291/month; WarrantyKhata's planned Rs 99-299/month overlaps.
