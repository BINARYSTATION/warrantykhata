## How the TAM was built (bottom-up, defensible)
150,000 shops x Rs 199/month x 12 = Rs 35.82 crore/year -> stated as Rs 36 crore.
- 150,000 = AIMRA's own site: "Over 1.5 lacs mobile retailers of India" (I fetched aimra.org myself).
  NOTE: AIMRA's own page says MOBILE retailers. A Sept 2026 news relay describes the same 1.5 lakh as
  "mobile phone and consumer-electronics retailers" - do not claim the broader wording as AIMRA's own.
- Rs 199/month = midpoint of OUR OWN planned Rs 99-299 pricing. This is an assumption, not a market fact.
  It must stay consistent with Module 6 pricing and Module 7 financials.
- Conservative on purpose: electronics-only shops (not in AIMRA) would add to this.

## Why not a published category number
- No India-specific warranty-software market size exists in any source checked.
- Grand View Research's India POS software figure (the best conceptual fit) was BLOCKED (403 + Cloudflare)
  and never actually read -> NOT USED. Never quote it as fact.
- India accounting-software market: IMARC says USD 0.70bn (2025), Maximize says USD 14.54bn (2025) - the same
  category and year, ~20x apart. Both paywalled beyond the summary. Neither dollar figure is trustworthy.
- "3 lakh mobile retail outlets" - a widely repeated figure that could NOT be traced to any source. NOT USED.
