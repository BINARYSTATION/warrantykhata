# Dukaan wale se baat — interview script (10-15 min)

**Yaad rakhna:** aap bechne nahi, **seekhne** gaye ho. Zyada suno, kam bolo. App ka zikr sabse aakhir me.
Shop busy ho to baad me aane ka time pooch lo. Dopahar ka dheema time best rehta hai.

## Shuruaat
"Namaste, main Manish. Maine 12 saal electronics ki dukaan pe kaam kiya hai, sales se leke accounts tak.
Abhi ek project pe kaam kar raha hoon ki dukaan wale warranty aur purane record kaise sambhalte hain.
Kuch bechne nahi aaya, bas 10 minute aapka experience samajhna hai. Chalega?"

## Sawaal (pichli baaton pe, "kya karoge" pe nahi)
1. Dukaan me kya-kya bechte ho? Din me lagbhag kitne customer? Staff kitna hai?
2. Warranty aur serial/IMEI ka record abhi kaise rakhte ho? *(Dikha sakte ho? Photo le sakta hoon?)*
3. **Pichli baar** koi customer warranty claim leke aaya, tab kya hua? Bill mila? Kitna time laga?
4. Pichle ek mahine me kitni baar kisi purani sale ka serial/IMEI dhoondhna pada?
5. Kabhi aisa hua ki record nahi mila aur customer se bahas hui, ya customer chala gaya?
6. Warranty khatam hone pe ya service ke time customer ko yaad dilate ho? Kaise?
7. Is kaam ke liye kabhi koi app, Excel ya software try kiya? Kya hua? Kyun chhoda?
8. Ye kaam kaun karta hai, aap khud ya staff?
9. Agar ye jhanjhat kal se khatam ho jaye, to aapka kya bachega: time, paisa, ya customer?

## Aakhir me (sirf ab idea dikhana)
10. "Main ek chhota test kar raha hoon: 2 hafte ke liye aapka warranty record main khud sambhalunga,
    free me, aur customers ko reminder bhi bhejunga. Karke dekhna chahoge?"
11. "Aapke jaan-pehchaan me aur koi dukaan wala hai jisse mujhe baat karni chahiye?"

## Mat poochna
- "Aap ye app use karoge?" (log sharam me haan bol dete hain)
- "Kitne paise doge?" (andaze ka jawab milega, sach nahi)
- Sawaal jisme jawab pehle se chhupa ho, jaise "Record khona bahut bura lagta hai na?"
